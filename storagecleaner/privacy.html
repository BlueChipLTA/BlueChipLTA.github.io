Storage Cleaner Privacy Policy

Storage Cleaner (shown as “Dọn dẹp bộ nhớ” in Vietnamese and “Очистка памяти” in Russian) helps you find duplicate and look-alike photos, screenshots, blurry photos, large videos, duplicate contacts and app sizes on your Android phone. This policy explains what the app reads, what stays on your device and which Google services the free version uses.

Photos, videos and contacts stay on your phone

With your permission, the app reads your photos and videos to group look-alikes, find exact duplicates, blurry photos, screenshots and large files, and to compress videos you choose. Photos are analysed on the device from small thumbnails. The app keeps a small local file with a fingerprint and a sharpness value for each photo so the next scan is faster. Compressed videos are saved to Movies › Storage Cleaner on your phone.

If you open Contacts, the app reads your contacts on the device to find duplicates and contacts without a number or email. Merging links duplicate contacts; it does not remove any number or email. If you allow Usage Access, the app reads how much space each app uses and when it was last used, only to show the App Sizes list.

Your photos, videos, contacts, fingerprints and app-size information are never uploaded to BlueChipLTA or to any advertising or analytics service. There is no account.

Deleting

Nothing is deleted without Android's own confirmation. Deleted photos and videos are moved to Recently Deleted, where Android keeps them for 30 days before removing them for good; you can recover them during that time. Background scans and reminders never delete anything.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can send a daily clean-up reminder, an alert when a new large video is saved, and an occasional screenshot reminder. These are created on the device from the local scan and contain only counts and sizes. You can turn each of them off in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform. Ads are never shown on the delete confirmation, the permission screens, notifications or widgets. When ads are used, Google may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers, interactions with the app or ads, and performance or diagnostic information, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where required, a consent message is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad Privacy Options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.

Analytics and crash reports (free version)

The free version uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used (for example screen views and app events) and Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and diagnostic information so we can fix problems. This data includes device and app information and app-instance identifiers. It never includes your photos, videos, file names, contacts or app list.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from partner apps and Firebase privacy information. These services may process data in countries other than your own; their retention and deletion options are managed by Google. Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit.

Purchases

Storage Cleaner Pro (weekly, yearly or one-time lifetime) is sold through Google Play. Google Play handles payment information; the app does not collect payment-card details. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Google Play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is meant for a general adult audience.

Your choices and deletion

You can deny or revoke the Photos and Videos, Contacts, Usage Access and Notifications permissions at any time in Android settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes all data the app kept on your device.

Contact and changes

For questions or privacy requests, contact bluechip.dev@gmail.com. We update this page when the app's data practices change, with the revision date above.
