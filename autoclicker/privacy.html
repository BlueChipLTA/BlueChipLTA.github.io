Auto Clicker Privacy Policy

This policy describes the rebuilt Auto Clicker Android app. Scripts and recorded gestures are stored on your device. The free public version can display Google AdMob advertisements, which involve the device and advertising data described below. A separate private testing version does not initialize Google advertising services.

Scripts, recording and accessibility

Auto Clicker uses Android Accessibility Service to perform taps and swipes at positions you configure. Recording starts only when you choose it. The recording overlay captures touch coordinates and timing, then replays the gesture after you lift your finger. It does not record screen images, microphone audio, text or the contents of other apps. The service is configured without permission to retrieve window contents.

Scripts, gesture coordinates, timing, preferences and imported legacy scripts stay in app storage. We do not upload them to BlueChipLTA or an advertising service. If you export a script and choose a storage or sharing destination, that destination handles the exported file under its own policies. You can delete scripts in the app, clear the app's storage, disable accessibility in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Advertising and privacy choices

The public free version uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform. Ads appear only on eligible foreground Home or Library screens. The app removes its advertisements before opening its floating automation controls. It does not use the accessibility service to interact with its advertisements.

When advertising services are used, Google may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device/account identifiers, interactions with the app or ads, and performance or diagnostic information. This supports advertising delivery and measurement, analytics within the advertising service, and fraud prevention. The app does not request GPS location for advertising. Advertising data is encrypted in transit. Google's handling and retention of its data are described in its own policies.

Where required, a consent message is presented before ads can be requested. You can revisit available choices through Settings → Advertising privacy (shown in Vietnamese as “Quyền riêng tư quảng cáo”) when required by the consent platform. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID using Android's advertising privacy settings. Changing these choices does not delete your scripts.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps. These services may process advertising data in countries other than your own. Their data retention and deletion options are managed by those providers.

Purchases and diagnostics

The app can ask Google Play for previously owned in-app products to preserve the old version's advertising-free access. Google Play manages purchase and payment information; the app does not collect payment-card details. The rebuilt version does not offer new purchases.

This build does not enable separate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics collection. This does not disable the advertising SDK's own diagnostic and measurement data described above.

Contact and changes

For questions, privacy requests or concerns about a child's use, contact bluechip.dev@gmail.com. Please do not send sensitive screen content or scripts unless necessary for your request. We update this page when the app's data practices change, with the revision date above.
